# Follow-ups to 0.7

The three follow-ups left open by 0.7, the 35B results for akm
0.10.261006-beta, and patch releases. No eval changed version, and no eval asks
akm anything new: saved runs compare as before.

## What changed

- **`generate --help`.** Every `generate` script (the nine evals that have a
  rewrite, and LongMemEval) prints its usage and exits 0 on `--help` or `-h`.
  An unknown flag, or a missing or invalid `--seed` or `--out`, prints the
  usage and exits 2. They used to stop with a stack trace.
- **`evals/retrieval/label` reads `chat`.** A judge that is a line of
  `models.json` takes that line's `chat` settings, as LongMemEval does:
  `temperature`, `max_tokens` or `max_completion_tokens`, and
  `reasoning_effort` replace the defaults of 0, `max_tokens` 2000 and `none`.
  Without them the requests are the same as before.
- **`eval_settings`.** The manifest field that holds an eval's own settings
  (consolidate and nightly's `embedder`, the Harbor evals' `attempts`) is now
  `eval_settings`; it was `treatment_hints`. New runs and `scripts/rescore`
  write the new name. Every reader (`scripts/compare`, LongMemEval's
  `--resume`, the manifest check) takes either name, so the published runs
  are read as they are, and none was rewritten. `manifest_version` stays 1.
- **Published results.** [`reports/0.10.261006-beta/`](../reports/0.10.261006-beta/README.md)
  holds every eval but LongMemEval and Terminal-Bench on the 35B with
  semantic search and full public sets, next to the 0.10.261005-beta runs.
- **Patch releases.** The release workflow takes `MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH` as well
  as `MAJOR.MINOR`.

## After upgrading

Nothing to do. A script of your own that reads `manifest.treatment_hints` from
a new summary should read `manifest.eval_settings`, and fall back to
`treatment_hints` for runs saved before 0.7.1.
