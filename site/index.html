Reading the results

  • Coverage: completed artifacts include pilots and diagnostics. Small samples do not estimate full-suite performance. Collection errors remain visible and can reduce a metric's denominator.
  • Repeat variation: the displayed flip rate compares recorded correctness, pass decisions or outcomes, as labelled. Only case IDs present and scorable in every run of the group are included. This display does not rescore artifacts. For controlled comparisons use the eval harness's compare command.
  • Versions: measurement versions are separate from AKM CLI and harness release versions. Changed collectors or fingerprints form separate groups. A higher score across different groups alone does not establish improvement.
  • Reflect: file-layout refusals and indexed-ref errors remain outside correctness when the original scorer excludes them. A high score over the remaining cases does not establish full compatibility.
  • Retrieval: inspect relevance-judgment coverage and condensed metrics. Notification and abstention observations are separate from judged task or evidence questions. Retrieval-only runs measure evidence ranking without an answer model.
  • Models: pooled serving does not establish a fixed quantization or hardware allocation. GPT-120 pilots affected by rate limits or answer cut-offs remain demonstrations; see their original report before comparing.
