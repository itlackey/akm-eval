What the numbers mean

Score and case count

A result such as 25 / 27 means 25 cases passed out of 27 scored. “44 of 50 scored” means six cases could not be scored. Errors stay visible beside the score.

Repeated runs

Details show each published repeat. A comparison uses runs with matching test inputs, code, model settings and AKM version. Changed test code is marked separately.

Search scores

nDCG measures how well relevant results rank, from 0 to 1. Recall measures how much relevant material was found. Higher is better for both; judgment coverage shows how much was graded.

Small trials

A few cases show whether a benchmark can run. They are listed separately from full tests, with compatibility problems and failed model calls shown in the details.
