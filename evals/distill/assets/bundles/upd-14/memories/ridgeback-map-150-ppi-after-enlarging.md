The vendor's preflight flagged the double-page map in the Ridgeback campaign
book at 150 ppi effective. The file itself was 300 ppi, but it had been
enlarged to 200 percent in the layout to fill the spread, which halves the
resolution. The vendor asks for 300 ppi at final size. The cartographer
exported the map again at the full spread size, and the next preflight passed.
