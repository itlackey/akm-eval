The developer onboarding guide was cut from 14 pages to 6 in August, mostly by
moving the history of the build system to an appendix. Of the four people who
joined in September, three finished their local setup on the first day and one
on the second; before, setup usually took two days. Four people is a small
group, and two of them had used the same stack at their last job, so the change
cannot be put down to the guide alone. Nobody has asked them what they read.
