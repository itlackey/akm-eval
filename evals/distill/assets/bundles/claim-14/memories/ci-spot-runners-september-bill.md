In September the CI runners moved from on-demand to spot instances. The runner
bill for the month was 38 percent lower than in August, with about the same
number of jobs. Two jobs were interrupted when their instance was reclaimed and
re-ran from the start, which added about 25 minutes to those two pipelines. One
month is all there is so far, and spot prices in the region were unusually low
in September according to the provider's price history.
