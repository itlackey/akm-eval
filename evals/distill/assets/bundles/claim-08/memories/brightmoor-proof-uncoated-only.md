To compare printers for the Ashgrove atlas, Brightmoor Print sent a proof of
the four-page colour test on uncoated 120 gsm paper. Laid next to the old
printer's copy under the viewing lamp, the greens and the map reds matched, and
the skin tones in the cover photo were slightly warmer. The atlas plates are on
coated 150 gsm paper, and Brightmoor has not proofed anything on coated stock
yet. No price has been agreed.
