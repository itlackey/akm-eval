The 3.4 release notes said only `Refactored the auth module`. The refactor
invalidated every session, so all users were logged out once after the upgrade,
and support got 40 tickets that morning from people who thought their account
had been locked. A note saying that everyone would have to log in again once
would have answered them. The 3.4.1 notes were written from the reader's side
and drew no tickets.
