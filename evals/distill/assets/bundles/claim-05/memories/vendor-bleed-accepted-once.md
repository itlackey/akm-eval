The Tallowmere campaign book PDF passed the print vendor's preflight with a
0.125 inch bleed and was accepted. The vendor's template page asks for 0.25
inch, and nobody asked them about the difference. It is possible that the
preflight only checks for a minimum of 0.125 inch and that the template page is
older than the check, but that has not been confirmed. The two earlier
Tallowmere books were built with 0.25 inch.
