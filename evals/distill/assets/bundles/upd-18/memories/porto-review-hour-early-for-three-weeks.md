The weekly review with the Halifax and Porto offices was created by an
organiser whose calendar is set to New York time. The US clocks went forward on
8 March and the European ones on 29 March, so for three weeks the meeting
started an hour earlier in Porto, and two people there missed it twice. Setting
the event's own time zone to Europe/Lisbon, where most of the people who attend
are, keeps it at 15:00 Porto time all year.
