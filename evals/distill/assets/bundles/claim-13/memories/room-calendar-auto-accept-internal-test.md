Bookings of the Fernhall meeting room used to wait for the office manager to
accept them. In the Workspace admin console the room's booking setting was
changed to accept invitations automatically when the room is free. One test
booking from a staff account inside the domain was accepted within seconds.
Nobody tried a booking from an external guest, and it is not known whether
recurring meetings, which the team uses for the weekly review, are accepted
too.
