Three pages of the Ashfall zine came back from the printer with a white
hairline along the outer edge. On those pages the background colour box had
been drawn to the trim and not past it, and the cut wandered by about half a
millimetre. Extending the boxes 3 mm into the bleed on all three pages fixed
the reprint. The other pages already used a background image that ran into the
bleed.
