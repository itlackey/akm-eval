---
description: "Documentation regarding the archival process for legacy supplier invoices within the Northgate system."
---

The Northgate platform relocates supplier billing documents that exceed a two-year age threshold to a dedicated storage location known as the archive share. This specific repository enforces strict read-only permissions to prevent unauthorized modifications. Data integrity is maintained through quarterly tape backup procedures. Within this structure, files are organized hierarchically, utilizing the unique supplier identifier alongside the calendar year for filing purposes. To retrieve or view these historical records, users must possess the specific 'finance auditor' role assigned to their account. This ensures that only authorized personnel can access sensitive financial data stored in this long-term retention area. The system automatically handles the migration process once the age condition is met, ensuring compliance with data retention policies without manual intervention. Regular backups guarantee that no financial evidence is lost due to hardware failure or accidental deletion. Access control is strictly managed via role-based authentication, preventing general staff from viewing archived financial details. This setup balances accessibility for auditors with security for the broader organization.
