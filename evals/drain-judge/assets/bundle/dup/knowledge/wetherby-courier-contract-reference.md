description: "Summary of Wetherby's logistics agreement with Northline Express, including pickup times, weight thresholds, claim windows, and renewal schedules."
---

Wetherby relies on Northline Express for all package deliveries. The daily collection window closes at 16:30 during operational days. Any shipment exceeding 25 kilograms must be processed as a palletized load and reserved twenty-four hours prior to dispatch. If a package goes missing, the company has a two-week period starting from the dispatch date to file a compensation request. This specific service agreement undergoes an annual review and renewal process every June.
