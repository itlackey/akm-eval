---
description: "Keep an EAN-13 barcode at 80 percent of nominal size or more."
---

The printer's prepress check fails an EAN-13 barcode printed smaller than 80 percent of its nominal size, because it may not scan; the Harrowby atlas cover failed with one at 70 percent. Place it at 100 percent and move the text around it. The barcode file itself was not the problem.
