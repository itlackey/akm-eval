---
description: "Protocol for collecting and processing water samples at Quarry Hill."
---

The facility conducts weekly aquatic testing on Mondays. Five distinct locations are visited to gather specimens. These fluids are immediately transferred into light-sensitive containers to prevent degradation. The temperature of the collection is maintained at 4°C during transit. Laboratory personnel must accept the deliveries before 14:00 on the scheduled day. If initial analysis reveals nitrate concentrations exceeding established safety thresholds, a follow-up investigation is mandatory. This secondary collection must occur within a 24-hour window from the first test. Strict adherence to these timing and handling requirements ensures data integrity and regulatory compliance for the site.
