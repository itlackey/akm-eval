---
description: "Summary of access, capacity, and storage protocols for the Ivybridge bicycle facility."
---

Entry to the Ivybridge bicycle storage area requires the identical credential used for the primary entrance. The facility accommodates a maximum of thirty two-wheelers, distributed across two distinct racking systems. If a bicycle remains stored over the weekend, staff must attach a written notice to the designated board. Cargo bicycles are restricted to the ground level rack exclusively. Emergency repair supplies, specifically spare inner tubes, are stored within a red container.
