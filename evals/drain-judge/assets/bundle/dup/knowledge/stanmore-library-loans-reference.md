---
description: "Summary of borrowing policies for the Stanmore staff library."
---

The Stanmore staff library permits patrons to borrow materials for a duration of four weeks. You are allowed to extend this period two additional times, provided no other individual has placed a hold on the item. Reference materials must remain within the designated reading area and cannot be checked out. There are no financial penalties for returning items after their due date; however, overdue copies will be publicly displayed on the notice board to alert other users.
