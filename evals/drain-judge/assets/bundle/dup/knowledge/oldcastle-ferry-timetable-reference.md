---
description: "Summary of the Oldcastle island staff ferry schedule, booking rules, and operational constraints."
---

Employees traveling between the mainland pier at Oldcastle and the offshore location utilize a specific boat service. Departures occur at three distinct times: 07:10, 12:40, and 17:30. Each journey requires twenty-five minutes to complete. To secure a spot, personnel must reserve their passage via the designated mobile application on the preceding day. The vessel is prohibited from sailing if wind speeds exceed force 7 on the Beaufort scale. Cyclists may transport their bikes on the lower deck without incurring any additional fees.
