---
description: "Summary of Thornley's biannual unannounced fire evacuation protocol."
---

Thornley conducts emergency evacuation simulations during the spring and autumn seasons, specifically targeting the months of April and October. Participants must gather at the southern parking area. Individuals designated as floor wardens are required to don yellow safety vests while inspecting their assigned levels. The procedure concludes only after the head warden collects signed confirmation lists from each floor, verifying that all personnel have evacuated. Crucially, these exercises occur without prior warning to the workforce, ensuring realistic response times and adherence to safety protocols during unexpected incidents.
