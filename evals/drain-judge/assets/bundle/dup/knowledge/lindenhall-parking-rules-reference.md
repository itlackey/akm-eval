---
description: "Summary of parking regulations and infrastructure details for the Lindenhall facility."
---

The Lindenhall location provides a total of forty-five designated parking spaces. Among these, four spots are reserved specifically for guest vehicles. Authorization for staff permits is handled by the reception desk, with all credentials requiring annual renewal during the month of January. For drivers operating electric cars, charging stations are installed in spaces numbered one through six. Access to the facility is granted via two methods: either through an automated license plate recognition system or by entering a specific code designated for visitors. The physical barrier mechanism responds to both of these authentication inputs to allow entry. It is important to note that the visitor allocation is fixed at four bays, distinct from the general pool. The permit renewal cycle is strictly annual, occurring in January, and does not happen automatically. Electric vehicle users must utilize the first six bays to access the charging infrastructure. The barrier system is dual-mode, supporting both visual plate scanning and manual code entry for temporary access.
