description: "Summary of the Harrow kitchen coffee maintenance schedule and supply chain details."
---

The responsibility for replenishing the espresso equipment in the Harrow office falls upon a rotating group of four staff members. Fresh coffee beans are sourced from the vendor known as Little Moth, with procurement occurring monthly on the first day. To ensure optimal performance, the facilities department performs a descaling procedure at six-week intervals. The current schedule for who handles this task is displayed on a sheet attached to the refrigerator door for easy access by all employees.
