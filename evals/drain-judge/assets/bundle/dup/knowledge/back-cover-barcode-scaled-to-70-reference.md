---
description: "Resolution of a prepress failure caused by an undersized EAN-13 barcode on a book cover."
---

A quality assurance scan rejected the rear page of the Harrowby atlas due to an unreadable ISBN symbol. The root cause was a design adjustment where the creator reduced the EAN-13 graphic to 70% of its standard dimensions to accommodate adjacent text. However, manufacturing guidelines mandate a minimum scale of 80% for reliable optical reading. Since the source vector file was correct, the issue was purely dimensional. To rectify this, the graphic was restored to full 100% scale. Additionally, the accompanying blurb was shifted upward by 6 millimeters to provide necessary clearance. This adjustment satisfied the printer's technical requirements, allowing the file to pass inspection without further modification to the barcode data itself.
