---
description: "Summary of commit message standards for the harborlight codebase."
---

Developers working within the harborlight ecosystem must adhere to a rigid structure for all version control entries. The required syntax follows the pattern `type(scope): subject`. The subject line demands the use of imperative verbs written entirely in lowercase letters, while strictly capping the length at sixty characters. A final period is prohibited at the end of this line. When a specific task or branch relates to an issue tracker, a dedicated footer labeled `Refs:` must appear on a separate line, followed by the unique ticket identifier, such as `HBL-417`. This protocol applies universally to every single commit made in the repository, regardless of whether the work occurs on a feature branch or the main line. Consistency is mandatory across the entire project history to maintain clarity and automation compatibility.
