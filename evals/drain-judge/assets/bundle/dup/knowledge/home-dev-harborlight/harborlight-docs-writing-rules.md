---
description: "Writing rules for harborlight documentation under docs/."
---

Text under `docs/` in harborlight is in British English (colour, behaviour, licence), with sentence-case headings, and talks to the reader as you. Avoid simply, just, easy and easily. Flag names stay exactly as the code defines them, `--color` included, while the prose around them keeps British spelling; `docs/guide/status.md` shows the style.
