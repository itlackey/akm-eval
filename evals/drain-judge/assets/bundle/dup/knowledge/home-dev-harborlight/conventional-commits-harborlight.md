---
description: "Commit message rules for harborlight."
---

Every harborlight commit uses `type(scope): subject`. The subject is in the imperative, all lower case, at most 60 characters, without a full stop at the end. When the work belongs to a ticket, put `Refs: HBL-417` (with the real ticket) on a line of its own as the footer. This holds on every branch.
