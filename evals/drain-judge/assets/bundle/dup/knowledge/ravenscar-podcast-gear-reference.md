description: "Inventory and operational details for the Ravenscar podcast audio capture setup."

This entry outlines the specific hardware configuration utilized by the Ravenscar podcast production team. Audio capture relies on a pair of dynamic-type microphones connected to a recording device featuring four distinct input channels. The resulting digital audio files are exported in the WAV format, utilizing a sample rate of 48,000 Hz and a bit depth of 24 bits. Physical storage for this equipment occurs within a grey protective case located inside the designated media cupboard. Additional power sources, specifically spare batteries, are stored in the front pocket of that same container. To track usage, staff must sign out the case using the official equipment sheet.
