---
description: "Guidelines for formatting content on the Ulverston wiki platform."
---

Contributors must adhere to specific structural rules when editing the Ulverston wiki. Each article requires an initial single-line overview at the top. All section titles should utilize sentence case capitalization rather than title case. When embedding code blocks, the programming language must be explicitly declared within the fence syntax. Visual assets, specifically screenshots, must be saved in PNG format and must not exceed a width of 1200 pixels. Furthermore, maintenance protocols dictate that any page lacking an assigned owner in its header metadata will be automatically archived after a period of six months. These standards ensure consistency and maintainability across the documentation repository. Adhering to these constraints helps keep the wiki organized and accessible for all users. Developers should verify that all images meet the dimension requirements before uploading. It is crucial to check for owner assignment to prevent unintended archiving of active content. By following these directives, the quality and uniformity of the wiki are preserved for future reference and collaboration.
