---
description: "Technical specifications and operational procedures for the Pinewood thermal labeling device."
---

The device located at the network endpoint pw-labels.internal handles thermal media with dimensions of 100x150 millimeters. Each supply roll contains exactly five hundred labels. To recalibrate the mechanism after replacing a roll, press and hold the calibration button for three seconds. Any defective prints are discarded into a designated bin. Staff must tally the quantity of these errors once per week, specifically on Fridays.
