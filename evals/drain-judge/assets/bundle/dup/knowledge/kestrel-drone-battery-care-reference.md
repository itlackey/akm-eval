---
description: "Guidelines for maintaining Kestrel survey drone power cells."
---

When storing the power units for the Kestrel aerial platform, ensure they remain at half capacity. After any flight operation, allow the cells to cool for twenty minutes prior to initiating a recharge sequence. If you observe any swelling in the casing, immediately place the unit inside the designated fire-resistant pouch and notify the safety officer. Each cell must be clearly marked with a unique identifier painted on its surface, which corresponds to an entry in the cycle tracking log. Once a battery reaches three hundred charge cycles, it must be taken out of service and retired. Proper adherence to these protocols ensures longevity and safety for all drone operations.
