---
description: "Summary of the Moorfield workshop safety assessment protocol."
---

Individuals initiating their tenure at the Moorfield facility must complete a mandatory evaluation consisting of twenty items. To achieve certification, candidates are required to answer at least eighteen questions correctly. Should an applicant fail this assessment, they are permitted to attempt it again following a three-day waiting period. Successful completion grants a two-year validity window, with the credential physically stored on the employee's access card. The content of these questions undergoes an annual review process during the spring season to ensure current standards are met.
