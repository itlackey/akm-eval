---
description: "Summary of seasonal restrictions, weather-based closures, and group size regulations for the Yewdale trail system."
---

Access to the lower section of the Yewdale path is prohibited during the winter months, specifically spanning from November through March. Additionally, the upper ridge route becomes unsafe and shuts down whenever wind speeds exceed a threshold of 60 kilometers per hour. Visitors should check the notice board located at the parking area for real-time updates regarding these restrictions. Furthermore, any organized party consisting of more than twelve individuals must obtain prior authorization from the site warden before entering the area. These rules ensure safety and preserve the natural environment during high-risk periods or for large gatherings.
