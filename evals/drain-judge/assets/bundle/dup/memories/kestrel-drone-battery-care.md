# Kestrel drone battery care

Kestrel drone batteries are stored at 50 percent charge. A battery that has been flown is left to cool for 20 minutes before charging. Swollen batteries go into the fireproof bag and are reported to the safety officer. Each battery has a number painted on it and a line in the cycle log. A battery is retired after 300 cycles.
