# Ravenscar podcast gear

The Ravenscar podcast is recorded with two dynamic microphones and a four channel recorder. Files are saved as 48 kHz 24 bit WAV. The kit is kept in the grey case in the media cupboard. Spare batteries are in the front pocket of the case. The case is signed out in the equipment sheet.
