# Oldcastle ferry timetable

The staff ferry leaves Oldcastle pier at 07:10, 12:40 and 17:30. The crossing takes 25 minutes. Seats are booked in the travel app the day before. No ferry runs when the wind is above force 7. Bicycles are carried on the lower deck at no charge.
