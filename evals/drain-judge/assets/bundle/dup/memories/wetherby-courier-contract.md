# Wetherby courier contract

Wetherby parcels go with the courier Northline Express. Pickup is at 16:30 on working days. Parcels over 25 kg are sent as pallets and booked a day ahead. Lost parcels are claimed within 14 days of the ship date. The contract is renewed in June.
