## Retired: Thornley Fire Drill Protocol

**Status:** Retired on March 15, 2026.

Previously, Thornley conducted unannounced fire drills twice annually (April/October). Staff assembled at the south car park while floor wardens in yellow vests swept their areas. Roll call relied on physical lists signed by floor heads, a process prone to delays and lost paperwork.

This approach was replaced by the **Digital Emergency Response System (DERS)** in Q1 2026. DERS utilizes real-time mobile check-ins and automated headcount algorithms, eliminating manual list errors and significantly reducing assembly time. This note is preserved solely for historical reference regarding our previous operational workflow.
