## Retired: Legacy Kestrel Battery Protocol

**Status:** Retired on March 15, 2026.

Previously, the team stored Kestrel drone batteries at a full 100% charge to ensure maximum readiness for immediate deployment. Flown batteries were charged immediately after landing, regardless of thermal state, which occasionally led to thermal runaway incidents. Swollen cells were discarded in standard waste bins, and batteries were tracked via a shared spreadsheet rather than individual cycle logs. This method was replaced by the **SafeCharge Initiative**, which mandates 50% storage, mandatory cooling periods, and dedicated fireproof containment for defective cells to enhance safety and longevity.
