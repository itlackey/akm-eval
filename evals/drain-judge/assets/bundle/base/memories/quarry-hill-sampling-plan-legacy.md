## Retired: Legacy Quarry Hill Sampling Protocol

**Status:** Retired on 2026-03-15

This note documents the obsolete procedure for sampling water at Quarry Hill, retained solely for historical reference. Previously, the team collected samples manually at five distinct points every Monday morning. The protocol required placing samples in amber bottles and immediately cooling them to 4 degrees Celsius to preserve integrity. Logistics were tight; the laboratory had to receive these samples by 14:00 on the same day to ensure valid analysis. If nitrate levels exceeded the regulatory limit, a mandatory second sample was taken within 24 hours.

This manual, time-sensitive approach was replaced by the **Automated Continuous Monitoring System (ACMS)** in March 2026. The ACMS provides real-time data, eliminating the need for physical transport and reducing human error. Refer to the current "Quarry Hill Water Sampling" note for the active workflow.
