---
description: "Retired legacy manual translation workflow for Juniper product terms."
tags: [i18n, glossary]
---

Prior to 2026, Juniper relied on a manual, spreadsheet-based translation workflow. Translators downloaded CSV files containing product terms, manually matched them against external reference documents, and uploaded the results. This approach was prone to version control conflicts and inconsistent terminology across regions. The process was officially retired on March 15, 2026, due to high maintenance overhead and frequent errors. It was replaced by the Juniper Translation Glossary, an automated system that integrates directly with the translation tool. This note is retained solely for historical reference to document the evolution of our localization strategy and avoid repeating past inefficiencies. The old method is no longer supported or accessible.
