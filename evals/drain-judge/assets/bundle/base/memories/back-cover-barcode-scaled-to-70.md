The printer's prepress check failed the back cover of the Harrowby atlas: the
ISBN barcode would not scan reliably. The designer had scaled the EAN-13
barcode to 70 percent to fit it beside the blurb. The printer's spec sheet asks
for at least 80 percent of nominal size for EAN-13. Placing the barcode again
at 100 percent, and moving the blurb up 6 mm to make room, passed the check.
The barcode file itself was fine; only its size was wrong.
