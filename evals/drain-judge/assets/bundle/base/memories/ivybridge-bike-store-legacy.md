## Retired: Ivybridge Bike Store Access Protocol

**Status:** Retired on March 15, 2026.

Previously, the Ivybridge bike store required a dedicated magnetic keycard for entry, distinct from the main building badge. This approach was abandoned due to frequent card reader failures and the logistical burden of issuing separate credentials to temporary staff. The team found the dual-system confusing for visitors and inefficient for maintenance.

**Replacement:** The store now utilizes the main building’s unified badge system. This change streamlined access control and reduced hardware costs. Historical records of the old keycard inventory are archived in the 2025 Q4 IT audit logs. This note is retained solely for historical reference to explain the transition to the current unified access model.
