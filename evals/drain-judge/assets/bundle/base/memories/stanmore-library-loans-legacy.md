## Retired: Manual Loan Ledger (Retired 2026-03-15)

This note preserves the history of the Stanmore staff library's previous loan management approach. Prior to March 15, 2026, the library relied on a physical ledger system to track book loans. Staff members manually recorded checkout dates, renewal counts, and due dates in a bound notebook. This method was prone to human error, difficult to audit, and inefficient for managing the growing collection.

The approach was officially retired on March 15, 2026, due to its scalability issues and lack of real-time availability data. It was replaced by the **Stanmore Digital Catalog System**, which automates loan tracking, sends automated renewal reminders, and provides instant visibility into book status. This legacy note is retained solely for historical reference and operational context.
