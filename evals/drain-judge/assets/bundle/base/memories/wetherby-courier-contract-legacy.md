## Retired: Wetherby Courier Contract (Legacy)

**Status:** Retired on March 15, 2026.

This note documents the historical logistics approach for Wetherby parcels, preserved solely for archival reference. Prior to March 2026, Wetherby utilized **SwiftShip Logistics** for all parcel deliveries. This provider offered next-day delivery with a 14:00 pickup cutoff. Claims for lost items required submission within 30 days. The contract was terminated due to inconsistent delivery windows and higher costs compared to the current provider.

**Replacement:** The team migrated to **Northline Express** to improve reliability and reduce shipping expenses. Northline Express provides a later 16:30 pickup window and more competitive rates for heavy items. This legacy note exists only to track the transition timeline and vendor history. Do not use these details for current operational planning.
