## Retired: Manual Permit Issuance

**Status:** Retired on March 15, 2026.

Previously, the team managed Lindenhall parking permits through a manual, paper-based workflow. Reception staff physically issued laminated permits to employees, which required renewal every January. This approach was prone to administrative errors, lost permits, and significant overhead during peak renewal periods. It also lacked real-time tracking of bay occupancy, leading to frequent disputes over EV charger access in bays 1-6.

**Replacement:** This process was replaced by the **AutoPark Digital System**. The new system automates permit issuance via a web portal, integrates with the barrier plate reader for seamless access, and provides real-time analytics for bay usage. This note is retained solely for historical reference regarding the legacy operational model.
