As of October 28, 2026, the Yewdale lower trail remains open for seasonal access, with the official winter closure scheduled to commence on November 1st. The ridge path wind monitoring sensors (v2.1) have been calibrated; however, the automated alert system is currently experiencing latency issues, delaying notifications when speeds exceed 60 km/h. Maintenance team has deployed commit a3f9b2c to patch the sensor firmware, but full integration is pending QA approval. The new digital permit portal for groups exceeding 12 people is in beta testing. We are targeting a live launch by November 15th to replace the manual warden check-in process. The physical information board at the car park has been updated with the new QR codes for real-time status checks. All stakeholders are aligned on the November 1st closure protocol.
