As of October 28, 2026, the Oldcastle ferry service remains operational with the standard 07:10, 12:40, and 17:30 departures. The 25-minute crossing time is accurate. However, recent updates to the travel app (build v4.2.1, commit a3f9c2b) have introduced a bug where bicycle reservations are occasionally double-booked. This issue is currently being tracked by the engineering team. Wind restrictions remain in effect for forces above 7. No changes to the timetable are pending for November. Staff are advised to monitor the app for updates regarding bicycle capacity limits. The next scheduled maintenance for the ferry hull is set for November 15, which may result in temporary service reductions. Please verify all bookings 24 hours in advance.
