## Retired: Ad-hoc Coffee Responsibility

**Status:** Retired on 2026-03-15

Previously, Harrow kitchen coffee maintenance lacked a formal structure. Team members were expected to refill the machine and order beans from Little Moth on an informal, rotating basis. This approach led to frequent stockouts and inconsistent machine maintenance, causing operational friction.

**Replacement:** The **Harrow coffee rota** replaced this system to ensure accountability and reliability. The new protocol assigns specific individuals to a four-person rotation, with beans ordered automatically on the first of each month and descaling handled by facilities every six weeks. This note is retained solely for historical context regarding our process evolution.
