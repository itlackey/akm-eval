## Retired: Analog Tape Recording Workflow

**Status:** Retired on March 15, 2026.

Previously, the Ravenscar podcast team recorded episodes using two analog tape decks and a reel-to-reel master recorder. Audio was captured on 1/4-inch magnetic tape at 15 ips. This approach was abandoned due to high maintenance costs, tape degradation, and inefficient post-production workflows. The physical media required constant cleaning and replacement, leading to frequent recording delays.

**Replacement:** The team migrated to the current digital setup using dynamic microphones and a four-channel digital recorder, saving files as 48 kHz 24-bit WAV. This shift improved audio fidelity, simplified backup processes, and eliminated the need for physical tape storage. This note is retained solely for historical reference to document the evolution of our recording infrastructure.
