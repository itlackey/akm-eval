## Retired: Paper Logbook Verification

**Status:** Retired on 2026-04-15

Previously, trail status was verified by physically checking the paper logbook at the Yewdale car park information board. This manual process was prone to human error and delays, as updates were not instantaneous. Staff had to manually transcribe closure notices into the central database, leading to inconsistencies between the physical board and digital records.

**Replacement:** This approach was replaced by the **Real-Time Digital Sync API**. This system automatically pushes closure data from the warden’s mobile app to the public website and mobile app, ensuring immediate accuracy. The paper logbook is now obsolete for real-time status checks, though it remains for historical record-keeping only.
