---
description: "Retired paper-based safety certification process for Moorfield workshop users."
tags: [workshop, safety]
---

Prior to 2026, new users at the Moorfield workshop were required to complete a physical, paper-based safety certification form. This manual process involved signing a hardcopy document that was then manually logged into the central registry by administrative staff. The validity period was also two years, but tracking renewals relied entirely on manual calendar checks, leading to frequent compliance gaps and administrative overhead.

This approach was officially retired on March 15, 2026. It was replaced by the **Digital Safety Badge System**, which automates quiz scheduling, grading, and access card updates. The legacy paper forms are retained in the archives solely for historical reference and audit trails, ensuring no data loss during the transition to the automated system.
