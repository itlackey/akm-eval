The recorded trial-gateway snapshot measured an upload ceiling of 32 MiB on the trial endpoint. A 33 MiB probe was rejected before storage while a 31 MiB probe succeeded, which localized the limit to the gateway. This is a historical observation, not a current configuration check. The present limit has not been established, and the snapshot must not be presented as a newly verified current value.
