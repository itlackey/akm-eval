# Contract Review Basics
Read the whole document before marking anything up, and confirm the parties,
recitals, and defined terms match the deal actually being made. Check for
the core commercial terms — price, scope, deliverables, timeline — and flag
any that are missing or vague enough to invite dispute. Pay close attention
to indemnification, limitation-of-liability, termination, and
dispute-resolution clauses, since these allocate risk rather than describe
performance. Watch for auto-renewal terms, unilateral amendment rights, and
non-compete or exclusivity provisions that may be broader than intended.
Confirm the governing law and venue clauses match the parties' expectations,
and verify that any referenced exhibits or schedules are actually attached.
