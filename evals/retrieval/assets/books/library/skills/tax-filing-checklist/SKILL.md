# Tax Filing Checklist
Gather income documents (W-2s, 1099s, K-1s), records of deductible expenses,
and last year's return before starting. Determine filing status and whether
to take the standard deduction or itemize. Reconcile any estimated tax
payments already made, apply eligible credits (child tax credit, education
credits, earned income credit), and compute the final balance due or refund.
Confirm the filing deadline for the current tax year, request an extension
if more time is needed to file (though an extension does not extend the time
to pay), and keep supporting documentation for at least three years in case
of an audit.
