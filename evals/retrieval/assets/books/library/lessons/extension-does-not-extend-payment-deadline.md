# Extension Does Not Extend the Payment Deadline
A client who files Form 4868 (or the equivalent extension request) gets
additional months to submit the paperwork, but the original payment deadline
does not move. Any tax owed and not paid by the original deadline continues
to accrue a failure-to-pay penalty and interest from that original date,
even though the return itself is not yet due. This is one of the most
common client misconceptions: they hear "extension" and assume the entire
obligation, including payment, has been pushed back. The correct advice is
to estimate the liability as accurately as possible and pay that estimate by
the original deadline, then true up the exact amount when the extended
return is actually filed. Failing to flag this distinction leads directly to
avoidable penalty exposure for the client.
