description: Keep watching for worsening signs well past the initial check on someone
when_to_use: Well past the initial check on someone, even when they look okay
lessonStrength: 5
---
Symptoms after a bump to the head can be delayed by hours, so seeming fine
right after a bump to the head does not rule out a problem. Keep monitoring
for delayed symptoms — worsening headache, repeated vomiting, confusion,
slurred speech, or unusual drowsiness — for at least 24 hours after a bump
to the head, and seek care promptly if any delayed symptom appears, rather
than assuming the initial "seems fine" check is the final word.

In practice: agree on a monitoring window with the person or a caregiver
before ending contact, and write down what to watch for after a bump to the
head so delayed symptoms are not missed hours later.
