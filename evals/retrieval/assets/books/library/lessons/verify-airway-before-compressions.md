---
description: Verify the airway is open before starting chest compressions, not after
when_to_use: When following the airway-breathing-circulation sequence during first aid or CPR
---
Starting chest compressions on someone with a fully obstructed airway wastes
critical seconds — circulation cannot help if no air can reach the lungs at
all once breathing attempts are made. Checking the airway first (and
clearing visible obstructions) before assessing breathing keeps the ABC
sequence in the order that actually matters for outcomes, rather than
jumping straight to the most visually urgent-seeming action.

In practice: open the airway (e.g. head-tilt/chin-lift when no spinal injury
is suspected) and glance for visible obstruction before spending the 10
seconds checking for normal breathing.
