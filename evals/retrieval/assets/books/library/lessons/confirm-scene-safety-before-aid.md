---
description: Always confirm the scene is safe before approaching someone who needs first aid
when_to_use: Before physically approaching or touching anyone in a first aid or emergency situation
---
A rescuer who becomes a second casualty (electrocution, traffic, structural
collapse, hazardous fumes) helps no one and often makes the situation worse
by adding a second person needing rescue. Scene safety is checked first,
every time, even when the visible injury looks urgent — a dramatic-looking
injury does not override an unsafe scene.

In practice: scan for hazards before moving toward the person, and if the
scene cannot be made safe, stay back and get emergency services routed to
the location instead of entering.
