---
description: Estimate federal income tax owed for the year given income, filing status, and deductions
---
Compute estimated federal income tax liability. Take gross income, subtract
above-the-line adjustments to reach adjusted gross income, subtract the
standard deduction (or itemized total) to reach taxable income, apply the
progressive marginal tax brackets for the filer's status, then subtract
applicable credits to reach the final estimated balance due or refund.
