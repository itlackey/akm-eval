# Plate Dessert

Turn a finished dessert component into a restaurant-style plated presentation.

1. Choose an odd number of elements (usually 3) for visual balance: a main
   component, a sauce or crumble for texture contrast, and a garnish.
2. Warm the plate slightly for warm desserts, chill it for cold ones — a
   temperature mismatch melts or fogs the plate.
3. Paint or dot the sauce first using the back of a spoon or a squeeze bottle,
   then place the main component off-center, not dead in the middle.
4. Add height with a quenelle (a football-shaped scoop of ice cream or cream)
   or a leaning tuile for visual interest.
5. Finish with a small garnish — a mint leaf, edible flower, or a dusting of
   cocoa/powdered sugar through a stencil — and wipe the plate rim clean before
   serving.
