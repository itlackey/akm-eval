# Meal Prep Week

Plan a full week of meals and batch-cook them in a single session to save time
on busy weeknights.

1. Pick 3–4 base proteins and 2–3 base grains/starches that reheat well
   (roasted chicken thighs, seared tofu, ground beef; rice, farro, roasted
   potatoes).
2. Roast or braise proteins in bulk on sheet pans or in a large Dutch oven so
   everything cooks in parallel rather than one pan at a time.
3. Cook grains in bulk (rice cooker, large pot) and roast a big tray of
   vegetables at high heat for caramelization.
4. Portion into airtight containers by meal, keeping sauces and dressings
   separate until serving so ingredients don't go soggy.
5. Label containers with contents and date; most cooked proteins and grains
   hold 3–4 days refrigerated, longer frozen.
