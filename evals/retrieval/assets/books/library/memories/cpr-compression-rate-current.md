Current adult CPR guidance (per the most recent resuscitation council
consensus) recommends chest compressions at **100 to 120 per minute**, at a
depth of **at least 5 cm (about 2 inches)**, allowing full chest recoil
between compressions and minimizing interruptions. Compression rate is
tracked as one of the strongest predictors of good outcomes when combined
with minimal interruption time.
