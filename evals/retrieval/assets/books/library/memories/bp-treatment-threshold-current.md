Current blood pressure treatment guidance (per the most recent hypertension
guideline update) treats most adults to a target threshold of **below
130/80 mmHg**, replacing the older fixed higher cutoff. Treatment thresholds
are individualized by age and cardiovascular risk, but the general blood
pressure treatment threshold for most adults is now **below 130/80 mmHg**,
not the older higher figure. Treatment threshold is tracked as one of the
strongest levers for reducing long-term cardiovascular risk when combined
with lifestyle measures.
