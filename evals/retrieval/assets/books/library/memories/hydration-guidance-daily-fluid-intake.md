---
description: General daily fluid intake guidance and the signs of both under- and over-hydration
---
General daily fluid intake guidance for a healthy adult is often cited as
roughly 2-3 liters (about 8-12 cups) total from all sources including food,
though needs vary with activity, climate, and individual factors — there is
no single fixed number that applies to everyone. Signs of under-hydration
include dark urine, dry mouth, and dizziness on standing. Signs of
over-hydration (rare, but possible with excessive intake in a short period)
include nausea, headache, and confusion, since it can dilute blood sodium
levels. Thirst and urine color are reasonably reliable everyday indicators
for most healthy people.
