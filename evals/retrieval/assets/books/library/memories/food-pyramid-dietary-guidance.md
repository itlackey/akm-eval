---
description: The old USDA food pyramid model for daily dietary guidance
beliefState: superseded
---
The original USDA "food pyramid" depicted daily dietary guidance as a
pyramid of stacked horizontal bands, with grains as the largest base layer
and fats/sweets at a narrow top, implying grains should dominate the diet by
volume. This model has been superseded by a plate-based model that instead
divides a single plate into proportional sections (vegetables, fruits,
grains, protein) alongside a dairy serving, which better communicates a
single-meal proportion than a stacked-pyramid abstraction did. Treat the
pyramid's implied "eat mostly grains" proportions as outdated relative to
current dietary guidance.
