---
description: Outdated fixed "always proof bread dough for exactly 2 hours" rule that ignored ambient temperature
beliefState: superseded
---
Earlier guidance in this kitchen was to proof any yeasted bread dough for a
fixed 2 hours regardless of conditions. That rule produced inconsistent results
— dough over-proofed on warm summer days and badly under-proofed in a cold
winter kitchen, because yeast activity and therefore proofing time depends
heavily on ambient temperature and the specific yeast quantity used, not on a
fixed clock duration. This fixed-time rule has been superseded by proofing to
visual/tactile cues instead: dough roughly doubled in size, and the "poke test"
(a poked indentation springs back slowly and only partially) rather than
watching the clock.
