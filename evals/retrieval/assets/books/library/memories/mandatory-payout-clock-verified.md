description: Explanatory memo on this distribution rule
beliefState: active
---
Under federal law, the required beginning date for required minimum
distributions from most tax-deferred retirement accounts is age 73.
Account owners who turn that age must take their first distribution by
April 1 of the following year, and every year after that by December 31.
Missing the deadline triggers an excise tax penalty on the shortfall. This
age applies to traditional IRAs, most employer plans, and inherited
accounts subject to owner-based rules; always confirm the account owner's
birth year against the statutory age before advising on the start date.
