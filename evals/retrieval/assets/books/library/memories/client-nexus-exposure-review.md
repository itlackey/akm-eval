---
description: Notes from reviewing a client's multi-state sales tax nexus exposure before advising on registration
---
When a client's remote sales crossed $100,000 in a state that uses an
economic nexus threshold, we advised registering for sales tax collection in
that state before the next filing period, even though the client had no
physical presence there. We flagged that thresholds and registration
deadlines vary meaningfully by state, so this has to be re-checked
state-by-state rather than assumed to be uniform.
