---
description: How we preserve attorney-client privilege in internal investigation witness interview memos
---
When drafting summaries of witness interviews for an internal investigation,
we mark each memo "Privileged & Confidential — Prepared at the Direction of
Counsel" and route it only through counsel first, since forwarding a
privileged memo directly to a broad non-legal executive distribution list
can waive the privilege. We keep distribution lists short and documented for
every draft.
