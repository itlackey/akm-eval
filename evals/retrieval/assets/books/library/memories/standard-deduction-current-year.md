---
description: Current-year standard deduction amounts we cite for individual filers
---
For the current tax year we use $15,000 for single and married-filing-
separately filers, $30,000 for married filing jointly, and $22,500 for head
of household, per the latest inflation adjustment. Add $1,950 (single) or
$1,550 per spouse (married) for filers who are 65 or older or blind. Always
confirm against the latest IRS revenue procedure before citing this figure
to a client, since it changes every year.
