---
Mise en place ("everything in its place") means finishing all prep work —
washing, chopping, measuring, staging ingredients in bowls in the order they'll
be used — before turning on any burner. Once heat is on, especially for a dish
with fast sequential steps (stir-fries, sauces that can break, anything with a
narrow doneness window), there usually isn't time to stop and chop an onion
mid-recipe without something else overcooking or burning. Doing the prep first
turns the actual cooking phase into pure execution against a plan, which is
also why `agent:sous-chef` treats prep-ahead sequencing as its first job when
organizing a service.
