Generalizing beyond wound care specifically: when a situation has both an
acute, escalating threat (active bleeding) and a slower-developing risk
(contamination/infection), address the acute threat first, then the slower
risk, and always check whether the treatment for one risk could itself
create a new harm (e.g. harsh antiseptic damaging healthy tissue while
"cleaning"). This ordering-plus-do-no-additional-harm pattern recurs across
first aid broadly, not just wound care.
