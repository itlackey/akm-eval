---
description: Store knives on a magnetic strip or in a blade-guarded block, never loose in a drawer
---
Knives kept loose in a drawer chip and dull far faster than knives stored
properly, because they knock against other utensils (and each other) every time
the drawer opens or closes — one chipped tip on a chef's knife took weeks of
sharpening to fully remove. Current practice is to store the main working
knives on a magnetic wall strip (blade never touching another blade) or in a
knife block, and to use blade guards for any knife that must travel in a bag or
drawer. This meaningfully reduces how often a full sharpening session (see
`skill:knife-sharpening`) is needed versus just honing.
