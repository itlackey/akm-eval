For the 2023 tax year we used $13,850 for single filers and $27,700 for
married filing jointly. This figure is now outdated: it has been superseded
by later-year inflation adjustments. Do not cite these amounts on a current-
year return; they are retained only for reference when reviewing a prior
2023 filing.
