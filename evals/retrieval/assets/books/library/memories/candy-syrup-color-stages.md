General notes on cooking sugar syrup for candy, distinct from onion
caramelization: the color moves through stages as cooking continues, gives a
pale straw color first and only much later gives a deep amber color, and
pushing further gives a bitter burnt result instead of a sweet caramelized
result, so a candy cook watches color and a thermometer rather than the clock.
