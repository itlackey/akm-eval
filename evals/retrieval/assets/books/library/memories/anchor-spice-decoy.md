A rambling pantry-organization note, not technique advice: whole spices, dry
goods, oils, and other pantry items all sit in labeled bins, and every so
often before a big cook someone should check whether the spices still smell
like anything or whether the flavor has faded, since a stale jar sitting on a
shelf for years does nothing to deepen flavor and grinding it won't bloom
much of anything back — better to toss it and buy a fresh pan-sized batch
than pretend a dry, dusty jar still helps a dish.
