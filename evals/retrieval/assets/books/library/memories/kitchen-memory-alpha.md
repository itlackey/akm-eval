Current best method for caramelizing onions: slice thin, use low heat (medium-low
at most) in a wide heavy pan with a little fat, and cook 35–45 minutes,
stirring every few minutes and scraping the fond as it forms. Patience is the
whole technique — real caramelization is a slow browning of the onion's
natural sugars, not a fast trick. Deglaze with a splash of water or stock
whenever the fond threatens to scorch, then let it build again. The result is
a deep amber color and a sweet, jammy flavor that a quick high-heat sear
cannot reproduce, because the sugars need time to break down evenly instead
of just charring the surface.
