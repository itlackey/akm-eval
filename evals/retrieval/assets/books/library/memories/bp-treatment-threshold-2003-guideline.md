An older (2003-era) guideline recommended treating blood pressure to a fixed
threshold of **140/90 mmHg** for most adults, without individualizing by
risk. This has been superseded by more recent guidance recommending a
**below 130/80 mmHg** target threshold for most adults, since outcome data
showed lower individualized treatment thresholds reduce cardiovascular
events. Do not use this fixed 140/90 figure as current guidance — see the
current-guideline memory for the active blood pressure treatment threshold.
