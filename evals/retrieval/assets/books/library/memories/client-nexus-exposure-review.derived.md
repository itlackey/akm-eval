General pattern inferred from the nexus exposure review: economic nexus
thresholds — commonly $100,000 in sales or 200 transactions in a state
within a year, though the exact figures vary by state — can create a sales
tax registration and collection obligation even when the seller has no
physical presence in that state. Multi-state sellers should track cumulative
sales by state against each state's specific threshold and register before
crossing it, since uncollected tax liability can accrue retroactively once
the threshold is exceeded, independent of any single client's facts.
