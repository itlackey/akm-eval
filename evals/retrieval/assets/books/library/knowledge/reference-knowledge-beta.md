# Toast Whole Spices Explained

Whole spices such as cumin, coriander, and mustard seed carry most of their
aroma in essential oils that stay locked inside the seed coat until heat
releases them. Toasting whole spices in a dry pan over medium heat for one to
two minutes, just until fragrant and a shade darker, blooms those oils before
grinding and produces a noticeably deeper, rounder flavor than grinding the
same spices cold.

The general technique: toast whole spices in a dry pan before grinding,
shaking or stirring constantly so they don't scorch, and grind them while
still warm for the best aroma. Pre-ground spice from a jar skips this step
entirely and tastes noticeably flatter side by side, because the oils have
already begun to fade.
