# Delayed Symptoms After A Blow To The Head

Someone who takes a blow to the head and seems fine at first can still
develop delayed symptoms hours later, which is why initial alertness does
not rule out a developing problem. General education monitoring windows
commonly cite roughly 24 hours during which delayed symptoms — worsening
headache, repeated vomiting, confusion, slurred speech, or unusual
drowsiness — may appear after a blow to the head.

## Monitoring Considerations After A Head Injury

- **Headache** that worsens rather than improves after a bump to the head.
- **Repeated vomiting** rather than a single episode.
- **Confusion or slurred speech** that develops or worsens over the
  monitoring window.
- **Unusual drowsiness** that is hard to rouse from.

This is general physiology/first-aid education content describing typical
delayed-symptom patterns after a bump to the head, not diagnostic guidance
for any specific case.
