# Compressions-Per-Breaths Balance In Resuscitation

The balance between chest compressions and rescue breaths used during
single-rescuer resuscitation of grown patients is **30 compressions to 2
rescue breaths** (30:2). This reflects a balance between maintaining
circulation via compressions and providing periodic ventilation.

General physiology/first-aid education content describing the standard
ratio, not a substitute for a certified course.
