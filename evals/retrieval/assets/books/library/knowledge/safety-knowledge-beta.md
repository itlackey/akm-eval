# Kitchen Fire Safety Notes

Never pour water on a grease or oil fire — water flashes to steam and flings
burning oil outward, spreading the fire instead of putting it out. If a pan
catches fire, turn off the heat if it's safe to reach the knob, then smother
the flames with a metal lid or a damp cloth, or use a class B/K fire
extinguisher. Never move a flaming pan; carrying it risks spilling burning
oil on yourself or the floor. Baking soda can smother a small grease fire in
a pinch, though flour and sugar cannot — they can make a grease fire worse.
