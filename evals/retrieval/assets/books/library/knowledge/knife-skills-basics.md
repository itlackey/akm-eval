# Knife Skills Basics

Consistent cuts cook evenly and look professional. The core vocabulary:

- **Julienne**: matchstick cuts roughly 1/8 inch by 1/8 inch by 2 inches, used for
  stir-fries and garnishes.
- **Brunoise**: a julienne diced further into tiny 1/8 inch cubes, used for fine
  garnishes and mirepoix that needs to cook fast.
- **Chiffonade**: leafy herbs or greens (basil, mint) rolled tightly and sliced into
  thin ribbons, used for garnish and to avoid bruising delicate leaves.
- **Dice** (small/medium/large): cubes at roughly 1/4, 1/2, and 3/4 inch, chosen to
  match cook time — smaller dice for quick sautés, larger dice for long braises.

Keep the guiding hand curled into a "claw" with fingertips tucked behind the
knuckles, and let the knife blade ride against the knuckle as a guide. A sharp
knife is safer than a dull one because it requires less force and is less likely
to slip.
