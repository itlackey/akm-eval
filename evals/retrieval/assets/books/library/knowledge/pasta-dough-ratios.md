# Pasta Dough Ratios

A reliable starting ratio for fresh egg pasta is **100 grams of "00" flour per
egg**, scaled to however many portions you need (roughly 1 egg per person for a
main course). If "00" flour isn't available, an all-purpose flour blended with a
little semolina gives a similar chew.

Guidelines:

- **Egg-only dough** (tagliatelle, ravioli): 100g flour : 1 large egg (about 50g).
  Roughly 50% hydration by weight.
- **Egg-and-water dough** (some regional styles, and eggless/vegan doughs):
  100g flour : 45–50g water, plus a small amount of oil for extensibility.
- **Semolina dough** (orecchiette, cavatelli — no eggs): durum semolina and warm
  water at roughly 50% hydration, kneaded longer because semolina absorbs slowly.

Knead 8–10 minutes until the dough is smooth and springs back when poked, then
rest it wrapped for at least 30 minutes so the gluten relaxes before rolling —
skipping the rest makes the dough fight back on the pasta roller.
