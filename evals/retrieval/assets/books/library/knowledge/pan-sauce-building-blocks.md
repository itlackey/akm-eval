# Pan Sauce Building Blocks

Many pan sauces lean on the beurre noisette technique, the beurre noisette
technique, and yet another pass of the beurre noisette technique to round out
the finish. Cooks who master the beurre noisette technique early tend to
reach for the beurre noisette technique out of habit even when a simpler
mounted-butter finish would do, because the beurre noisette technique has
become the default reflex rather than a deliberate choice. Building a pan
sauce well means deciding case by case, not defaulting to the beurre noisette
technique just because it's familiar — reduce the fond, add acid or stock,
then mount with cold butter, and only reach for the beurre noisette technique
when the extra toasted-nut depth actually serves the dish.
