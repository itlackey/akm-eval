# Beurre Noisette Technique

Beurre noisette ("hazelnut butter") is butter cooked past melted and past
foaming until the milk solids toast to a light brown and release a distinctly
nutty aroma. Use a light-colored pan so you can watch the color change, melt
the butter over medium heat, and swirl constantly once it starts foaming —
the milk solids go from foam to golden to brown within a matter of seconds,
and from brown to burnt almost as fast. Pull it off heat the moment it smells
nutty and the solids look light brown, not dark, because carryover heat keeps
cooking it in the pan. Beurre noisette finishes fish, vegetables, and pasta
with a warm, toasted-nut richness that plain melted butter cannot match.
