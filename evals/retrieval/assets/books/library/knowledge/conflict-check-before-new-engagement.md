---
description: General doctrine on why firms run a conflict check before opening any new client matter
---
Before opening any new client matter, firms run a conflict check across
current and former clients, adverse parties, and related entities. No
engagement letter goes out and no substantive work begins until the
conflict check comes back clear or any conflict is waived in writing. This
applies to every new matter regardless of practice area, and it applies
even when the prospective client is referred by an existing client.
Skipping the conflict check before opening a new matter exposes the firm
to disqualification and malpractice risk.
