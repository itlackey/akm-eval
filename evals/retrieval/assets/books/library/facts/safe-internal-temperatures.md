# Safe Internal Temperatures

Standing food-safety reference for doneness targets (USDA-style guidance):

- **Poultry** (whole or ground chicken/turkey): 165°F (74°C).
- **Ground meat** (beef, pork, lamb): 160°F (71°C).
- **Whole cuts of beef, pork, lamb, veal** (steaks, chops, roasts): 145°F (63°C),
  plus a 3-minute rest.
- **Fish**: 145°F (63°C), or cook until opaque and flakes easily.
- **Eggs and egg dishes**: 160°F (71°C) for dishes; eggs cooked until yolk and
  white are firm for standalone eggs.
- **Leftovers and casseroles**: reheat to 165°F (74°C).

Carryover cooking during a rest (see `lesson:rest-the-meat`) typically raises
internal temperature another 5–10°F for larger roasts, so it is normal and safe
to pull a large roast a few degrees under target.
