---
description: House convention for citing tax figures together with their source revenue procedure and tax year
category: convention
---
Whenever we state a dollar figure — a deduction amount, a phase-out
threshold, a contribution limit — we cite the specific IRS revenue
procedure and tax year it comes from, for example "Rev. Proc. 2024-40, tax
year 2025." This keeps advice auditable, makes it immediately obvious when a
cited figure needs to be refreshed for a new tax year, and prevents an
outdated number from silently circulating in client-facing notes.
