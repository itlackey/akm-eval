# Standard Compression To Ventilation Ratio For Adult CPR

The standard compression to ventilation ratio for adult CPR performed by a
single rescuer is **30 compressions to 2 rescue breaths** (30:2). This is
the standard ratio for adult CPR taught in most basic life support courses.
