# Pellam kiosk update

Pellam kiosks check for an update every night at 03:15 local time. An update is downloaded in the background and applied at the next restart. Kiosks restart themselves at 04:00 when an update is waiting. A kiosk that fails its self-test after an update goes back to the previous version. The self-test checks the card reader, the printer and the network. The update server is pellam-updates.internal.
