# Wexcombe room booking

Wexcombe rooms are booked in the calendar tool under the Rooms group. A booking is at most 3 hours. A room can be booked up to 60 days ahead. A booking that nobody checks in to within 15 minutes is released. The large room seats 16 and the small rooms seat 4. Recurring bookings need the approval of the office manager. Catering for the large room is ordered through the front desk 2 days ahead.
