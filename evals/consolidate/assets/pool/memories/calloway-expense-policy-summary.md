# Calloway expense policy

Calloway staff file expenses in the finance portal within 30 days. Receipts are required for every claim over 25 euros. Train tickets are claimed in second class. Meals while travelling are capped at 40 euros a day. Claims are paid with the next monthly salary. The manager approves a claim before finance sees it. Foreign currency claims use the purchase day rate.
