# Norwood invoice rules

Norwood invoices are numbered NW-year-sequence, such as NW-2026-00412. The sequence restarts at 1 on January 1. An invoice is issued on the last working day of the month. Payment is due 30 days after the issue date. Amounts are rounded to the cent after the tax is added, not before. A credit note gets its own number from the same sequence. Invoices are sent as PDF and are kept for 10 years.
