# Studio kiln firing

Kiln 3 is fired every Tuesday. The firing reaches 1060 degrees. Pieces must be dry on the day before the firing. The kiln log is kept on the clipboard at the door. The kiln is serviced by the studio technician each spring. Glaze is mixed on the day before the firing. A cooling kiln is never opened before it falls under 100 degrees. Damaged shelves are reported to the studio manager.
