# Oakhurst shift swaps

Oakhurst staff swap a shift by posting it on the board in the Oakhurst rota app. A swap needs a taker who is trained for the station. The manager approves a swap at least 24 hours before the shift starts. A swap that would give someone more than 48 hours in a week is refused. Night shifts can only be swapped for night shifts. A shift that nobody takes by 12 hours before the start goes back to the manager. Swaps are counted in the monthly staffing report.
