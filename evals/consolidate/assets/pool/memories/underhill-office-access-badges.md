# Underhill office access

Underhill staff enter the office with a badge. Visitors sign in at the front desk and wear a visitor sticker. The building is open from 07:00 to 20:00 on working days. A lost badge is blocked by the facilities team within 1 hour of the report. A new badge now takes 1 working day to arrive, since the printer moved in-house. The server room needs a second badge and a PIN. Badge logs are kept for 90 days.
