# Pinewood label printer

The Pinewood label printer prints 100 by 150 mm thermal labels. Labels come in rolls of 500. The printer is reached at the address pw-labels.internal. A roll change needs the calibration button held for 3 seconds. Misprints are collected in the bin and counted every Friday.
