# Jessamy on-call handover

The Jessamy on-call shift changes every Monday at 09:30 local time. The outgoing engineer writes the handover in the pinned thread in #jessamy-oncall. The handover lists open incidents, silenced alerts and planned changes. Silenced alerts older than 3 days must be unsilenced or ticketed before the handover. The incoming engineer replies with a thumbs up to confirm they hold the pager. Until that reply the outgoing engineer keeps the pager. The pager schedule itself is edited in the Jessamy rota tool.
