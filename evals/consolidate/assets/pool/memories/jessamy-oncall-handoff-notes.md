# Jessamy on-call handover

The Jessamy on-call shift changes each Monday at 09:30 local time. The outgoing engineer posts the handover in the pinned thread in #jessamy-oncall. The handover covers open incidents, silenced alerts and planned changes. Silenced alerts older than 3 days must be unsilenced or ticketed ahead of the handover. Until that reply the outgoing engineer keeps the pager. The incoming engineer replies with a thumbs up to confirm that they hold the pager. The pager schedule itself is changed in the Jessamy rota tool.
