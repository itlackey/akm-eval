# Jessamy on-call handover

The engineer going off shift writes the handover in the pinned thread in #jessamy-oncall. The Jessamy on-call rotation changes every Monday at 09:30 local time. The handover lists open incidents, silenced alerts and changes that are planned. Alerts silenced for more than 3 days must be unsilenced or ticketed before the handover. The engineer coming on replies with a thumbs up to confirm they hold the pager. Until that reply the outgoing engineer holds the pager. The pager schedule itself is edited in the Jessamy rota tool.
