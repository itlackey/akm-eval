# Bramwell greenhouse climate

The Bramwell greenhouses are kept at 22 degrees by day. At night the target is 16 degrees. Humidity is held between 60 and 75 percent. The vents open when the temperature passes 26 degrees. Shade screens close when the light passes 70000 lux. The climate controller logs a reading every 5 minutes. The tomato house is held 2 degrees warmer than the others.
