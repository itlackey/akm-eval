# Thornley fire drill

Thornley runs a fire drill twice a year, in April and in October. The assembly point is the south car park. Floor wardens wear yellow vests and sweep their floors. The roll call is complete when the head warden has the list signed by every floor. The drill is not announced to staff in advance.
